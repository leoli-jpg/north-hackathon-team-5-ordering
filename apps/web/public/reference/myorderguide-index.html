Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you let an AI pick dinner.

What is MyOrderGuide?
+
MyOrderGuide is an AI-powered food recommendation app that tells you exactly what to order from real nearby restaurants. Instead of scrolling endless menus, you get 3-5 specific dish picks chosen for your location, dietary goals, and the moment you're in.
Is MyOrderGuide free?
+
Yes. MyOrderGuide is free to use. You enter a location, pick a mode, answer one short question, and get personalized dish recommendations — no signup required.
Does it work for vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, or other restrictions?
+
Yes. Open Preferences to set dietary restrictions and allergies. Allergies are treated as hard constraints the AI will never violate. Vegan and vegetarian preferences shape both the restaurant search and the dish picks so every recommendation is safe to order.
Does MyOrderGuide store my location?
+
We use your location to find restaurants nearby. If you save a location while signed in, it's tied to your account so it's there next time. If you're browsing without signing in, your location stays in your browser. Either way, you can clear or change it anytime from the location input.
